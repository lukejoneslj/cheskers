    HOW TO PLAY

    The board. Your back rank is a normal chess set. In front of it stands a rank of checkers, not pawns.

    Chess pieces move and capture exactly as they do in chess.

    Checkers step diagonally forward and capture by hopping an adjacent enemy — any enemy, including a queen — onto the empty square beyond. Land another hop and you keep going in the same turn.

    Crowning. A checker reaching the far rank is crowned and may then move backwards. Crowning ends your turn, even mid-hop.

    Winning. There is no check and no checkmate. Capture the enemy King and the game is over, so guard yours yourself.

    Forced jumps. With this rule on, if any hop is available you must take one — even if you would rather move a chess piece.
